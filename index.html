An intensive test of economic strategy

Trinity is a single-day championship built around one continuous arc: teams break down a live business case, defend a strategy in front of judges, then carry that position into direct economic debate against opposing delegates.

Format

Three stages, one continuous flow from analysis to argument.

01

Business Case Solving

Teams analyze a complex, real-world business puzzle under strict time constraints — building a defensible strategy from raw financial and market data.

02

Strategic Pitching

Each team delivers a high-impact presentation to a judging panel, defending the reasoning and trade-offs behind their strategy.

03

Economic Debates

Teams face off directly, defending their position under live cross-examination in structured economic debate rounds.
